Small gold bars don't always stay in their original packaging, and a loose bar tends to end up in a bag or a drawer.

ReSheath is a simple card you seal yourself, so a loose bar has a proper home again. Leave your email and we'll let you know when it's ready.

How it works

Set your bar in the clear blister, line up the adhesive back, and press to seal.
